Dispatched work keeps its context carrier on the worker runtime.

- core: a dispatched workflow turn carries the engine's `context` carrier (`WorkflowTask.context`), as a dispatched step already did. `WorkflowExecutor.dispatch` takes an optional `{ context }`. The engine keeps the carrier it stamped on each turn in memory (bounded) and applies that turn's decision under it and in the run's tenant, so the steps and children the turn dispatches carry the same carrier. It never reads a carrier back from a worker; a decision applied by another instance, or after a restart, gets that engine's own context. A `rehydrate` that rejects the carrier is logged and the decision applied without it. Trace continuity is first-turn only: a later turn (after a step result, a signal or a timer) carries a fresh carrier from wherever it is dispatched.
- worker: `new DurableWorkerRuntime({ rehydrate })` restores a dispatched turn's or step's carrier around its replay or handler, outside the durable execution wrappers (which now also receive `context` for a turn). A turn whose carrier `rehydrate` rejects runs without it and is logged (the carrier is correlation only, so a changed `deserialize` during a rolling deploy does not fail runs); a step whose carrier is rejected fails retryable (`context_rehydrate_failed`) without running its handler, so its retry policy re-dispatches it with a freshly stamped carrier. The core peer floor rises to 0.107.0.
- nestjs: the auto-fed carrier is built with nestjs-context's `Context.serialize()` when its own accessor is bound, so the app's `carrier: [...]` allowlist or custom `serialize` decides what reaches the broker (the full `tenant` is added only on the allowlist path, when `tenantId` is allowed). The co-located and thin worker runtimes take the same `Context.deserialize` bridge the engine uses around a local step, when a context accessor is bound; what a handler sees is what your `deserialize` restores. Peer floors: `@dudousxd/durable-worker` 0.24.0, `@dudousxd/nestjs-durable-core` 0.107.0.

**Migration:** this changes what a dispatched task carries. If you set `carrier: [...]` or a custom `serialize` in `ContextModule.forRoot`, include `tenantId` (and `userRef`, if handlers read it), or remote step handlers no longer receive them: before, durable read them from the accessor regardless of the allowlist. In the other direction, any extra field nestjs-context's `serialize` emits (for example `tenantFamilies` in newer versions) now travels too.
