Durable console accent moves from emerald `#34d399` to lime `#a3e635`.

Emerald *is* `--good`. Using it as the accent gave one hue two jobs — "this step succeeded" and "this is interactive".

Durable picked last and had the least room left on the wheel, because its own local hues consume space too: bad 0°, no-worker 25°, warn 45°, good 160°, media cyan 187°, live 217°, sleeping 239°, agent violet 258°, telescope magenta 300°, dead 347°. Only two arcs survived. Lime sits mid-arc at 82° — 37° from `--warn`, 78° from `--good`.

Pink `#f472b6` separates further and was rejected on purpose: a pink primary action sits on the same row as a red FAILED badge and borrows an alarm it does not mean.
